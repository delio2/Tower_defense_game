# 02 — Monetization, rewards and marketing (v2)

> Version 2 · 2026-09-21 · Status: principles decided; the model (A or B) is chosen before soft launch with data.
> Principle: **"You pay to get there sooner or for convenience, never to win."** Permanent progression unlocks **variety** (modules and Cores), not power. Competitive modes use a **fixed kit**: no purchase can affect leaderboards, leagues or duels.
> Detailed economy and pricing: `08`. Launch marketing and store listing: `10` §3.

---

## 1. Fixed rules
- ❌ No energy, no waiting timers, no paid random boxes.
- ❌ No forced ads (interstitials, banners, ads at start-up).
- ❌ No content that gives **power** for money.
- ✅ Complete offline play. Monetization never blocks the game.
- ✅ Two currencies only: **Credits** (inside the run, never purchasable) and **Blueprints** (unlocks).

## 2. Two possible models (decision before soft launch, with data)
| | **A — Trial + unlock** (Ball x Pit / Balatro model) | **B — Fair free-to-play** |
|---|---|---|
| How | Act 1 and the Daily are free; **one-time unlock** of the full game (about €4.99–7.99) | Everything free; ads **only optional**; pass and purchases to **speed up unlocks** and for cosmetics |
| Pros | Cleanest positioning ("no ads, no tricks"); in line with the trend (+77% paid games in 2025) | More downloads; continuous revenue; fits seasons |
| Cons | Fewer downloads; revenue concentrated at launch | Needs careful balancing; risk of looking "like the others" |
| Fits asynchronous multiplayer | yes (leaderboards and ghosts for everyone, including trial players) | yes |

**Planned test:** soft launch in two similar countries, one per model, or an A/B test, comparing revenue per install and store rating (`10` §4, month 6).

## 3. If we choose B: the elements
**Optional rewarded ads, about 8 per day, never in competitive modes:**
| Working name | Moment | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Double Loot** | end of run | Blueprints ×2 |
| **Second Chance** | defeat | resume from the start of the lost wave (1 per run) |
| **Free Reroll** | shop | one free reroll (1 per shop) |
| **Daily Crate** | once a day | bonus Blueprints |

**Season Pass** (about €4.99 per 30 days, no auto-renewal): ad-free rewards (same limits), **Blueprints ×2** (faster unlocks), reward track with the season's cosmetics (Core and ring themes). Launched only with **D30 ≥ 7%** and a positive test. Structure in `08` §3.

**Other purchases:** lifetime VIP (no ads, about €7.99) · Blueprint packs (capped, `08` §2.2) · cosmetic themes. **None of these touches competitive play.**

## 4. Rewards and daily return
- **Daily Run** with leaderboard and ghosts: the main reason to come back (`10` §1).
- **Weekly Run** with special rules; **Leagues** from update 1 if the gates open (`10` §2.4).
- 3 simple daily missions (for example "win an act with 2 Amplifiers").
- Streaks without punishment; achievements; the collection of Cores and modules.

## 5. Marketing: what others get wrong and what we do
| Common mistake | Our approach |
|---|---|
| Misleading ads | **Real gameplay only** |
| Promising "free" and delivering pay-to-win | "**No waiting, no pay-to-win, offline**", stated clearly (never naming competitors, D20) |
| Marketing only at launch | Build in public from day one |
| One channel only | Store (ASO) + short videos + Reddit + Discord + creators |

### The idea's advantage
- **Build clips:** "from 10 damage to 10 million in 12 minutes", the ring filling up and numbers rising. Perfect for 15 seconds.
- **Player challenges:** "beat my ghost" with a seed code or link. Natural sharing.
- **Ring screenshots:** a build is understood in one image, and starts discussions ("is this combo broken?").

### Plan by phase (detail in `10` §3)
- **Prototype (now):** short devlogs on TikTok, Shorts, Reddit; Discord for the future 12 testers.
- **Vertical slice:** Google Play pre-registration; Indie Games Accelerator/Festival applications; contact roguelite and tower-defense creators.
- **Soft launch:** €300–1,000 on Google Ads using the best organic videos; measure D1, D7, D30 and revenue per install; test models A and B.
- **Launch:** ASO (keywords: "tower defense", "roguelike", "offline", "no pay to win", "idle defense"… **never other games' names**).
