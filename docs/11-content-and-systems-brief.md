# 11 — Content and systems v2 (roster, shapes, spawns, waves, rewards, economy)

> Version 1 · 2026-09-22 · Status: **PROPOSAL** brief (Phase 2.5 design, D31). Everything here extends or changes `05`; nothing is in `05` until approved, bot-tested and logged in `04`. Numbers use the `05` §3 units (range in units, cooldown in ticks, 60 = 1 s) and are **v0, to tune with the balance bot**.
> Visual reference: the *Roster*, *Spawn gates*, *Wave rhythm* and *Economy* cards in the Dusk Garden design system (https://claude.ai/artifact/5aKRr9H8e3WJsMui64MNnu).

---

## 0. Why this brief
The user approved the look (2026-09-22) and asked for four things before art production:
1. **Simpler names** and clearer concepts.
2. **Defined shapes** for every module and enemy, and **many more modules** before launch (14 today; `05` §6 already targeted ~40).
3. More **enemies**, and a clear answer to **where they come from**.
4. Clear **wave, reward and economy** mechanics.

Two problems surfaced while doing it, and this brief solves them too:
- **Pool dilution.** With 36+ modules in one shop pool, duplicates become rare and merging — the core joy — almost disappears. → *Run pool* (§6.2).
- **Random directions make placement meaningless.** Range is measured from the petal, so a module covers its own side better; today's random-per-enemy directions average that away. → *Gates and sectors* (§3).

## 1. Simpler names

### 1.1 Rules for every player-facing name
- One common English word, **≤ 8 letters**, that says what it does or what it looks like. No invented compounds (Swarmlet), no jargon (Credits vs Blueprints vs Archive).
- One concept, one word, everywhere (UI, Codex, store text). Code identifiers may keep old names until a rename commit.
- Levels are **stars** (★ ★★ ★★★), not "L1–L3": universal and wordless.

| Today | Proposed | Why | Alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| Integrity | **Health** | everyone knows it | Life |
| Slot / Ring position | **Petal** | it *is* a petal on screen | Slot |
| Module (in UI) | **Module** (keep) | neutral; the three families carry the meaning | Piece |

| Credits | **Coins** | the icon is a coin; shorter | Credits |

## 2. Shape grammar and the launch roster

### 2.1 The grammar
Every model is readable from the 35° camera at 0.85 units in three layers, **family → role → stars**:
| Layer | Attack (teal) | Boost (gold) | Support (mint) | Enemy (coral / rose) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Family = base form** | pointed crystal, vertical, sharp tip | ring or disc, open centre | solid, rounded, closed | angular shard, no curves |
| **Role = one added feature** | cluster · needle · orbit ring · long bar · squat · prism | teeth · gap · dot · double · ticks · crown | coin · shell · dome · pillar · drop · stack | crack · halo · tail · spikes · hollow · hook |
| **Stars = faces** | ★ base · ★★ + a smaller twin face · ★★★ + a third face and a gold edge | same | same | elite = double halo (never a new colour) |
| **Size** | 0.85 units (modules all the same) | 0.85 | 0.85 | size = toughness: 0.3 Mite → 0.6 Brute → 1.0+ Guardian |
| **Height** | tall | low (ring lies at 20°) | medium | floats at 0.45, speed = elongation |

Rules: two modules in the same family must differ in **outline**, not in detail; no faces, eyes or mascots; every shape is generated by a parametric Blender script (one per family) so new modules cost minutes.

## 3. Where enemies come from: gates and sectors

### 3.1 Gates
- A **gate** is a point on the arena edge (radius 9.0) in one of the 192 directions. Enemies of that gate enter within ±4 direction steps (±7.5°) and walk straight at the Core (Weaver excepted).
- **The shop shows the next wave's gates**: faint coral rifts on the arena edge and arcs on the spawn compass (design system *WavePreview*). The player places modules knowing the threat.
- **How many:** act I waves 1–2: 1 gate, waves 3–5: 2 · act II: 2–3 · act III: 3–4 · Guardian: its own gate + escort from 2.
- **Where:** seeded (Waves RNG stream), at least 48 steps (90°) apart. One **main gate** per act persists across the act's waves (so placement investments last); the others change every wave. In act III wave 5 the gates **drift** clockwise 1 step every 2 s.
- **Bursts:** each gate releases its share of the wave budget in **3 bursts** (t = 0, 7, 14 s) instead of a uniform drip. A new enemy type still enters alone first. Bursts make the Pulse a timing decision.

### 3.2 Sectors
- Each petal owns the **sector** in front of it: 360° ÷ number of petals (60° with 6), centred on the petal's direction.
- Range already counts from the petal (`05` §3), so a module naturally covers its own sector best. Sector-only modules (Barrier, Beacon, Magnet) make it explicit.
- Shown only when useful: while dragging or long-pressing a module, its sector draws as a soft wedge (never a permanent range circle, D20).

### 3.3 Why gates
They turn "enemies from every side" from noise into a readable question ("two gates: north-east and south — cover both or stack one?"), give placement lasting value, and make directional modules possible. The Daily and replays stay fair: gates are part of the seed.

## 5. Waves

### 5.1 The act rhythm (same six beats every act, so the player can plan)
| Wave | Beat | Composition | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Opening** | standard mix, fewest gates | warm-up, test the last shop |
| 2 | **Swarm** | small types, count ×1.25, HP ×0.85 | "do you have multi-target?" |
| 3 | **Themed** | one type only: the act's newest enemy | learn one question |
| 4 | **Siege** | heavy types, count ×0.7, HP ×1.3 (+ mutator from act II) | "are your hits heavy?" |
| 5 | **Crest** | full mix + elites (+ mutator from act II) | the pre-boss exam |
| 6 | **Guardian** | Guardian + escort at half budget | the act exam, then a Boon |
Budget and HP growth stay as in `05` §9 (budget ×1.10, HP ×1.12 per wave); the beat multipliers are applied on top.

### 5.2 Mutators (act II–III, at most one per wave, shown in the preview)
| Mutator | Rule |
|---|---|
| Armored | all enemies +1 armor |
| Hasty | +20% speed |
| Dense | +25% count, −15% HP |
| Shrouded | enemies are untargetable beyond 6.0 from the Core |
| Split | +1 gate |
The Weekly Run picks its mutators from this list.

### 5.3 Wave end
Order: last enemy dies → rings breathe → summary (Coins, damage share) → shop. After a Guardian, a **Boon choice** comes between the summary and the shop.

## 6. Rewards and economy

### 6.1 Coins per wave (in the run)
| Source | Coins | Change vs `05` §10 |
|---|---|---|
| Wave cleared | 4 | same |
| Interest | 1 per 5 held, max 5 | same |
| **Flawless** (no Health lost this wave) | +1 | new: rewards defence, not just damage |
| **Elite defeated** | +1 each | new: elites become risk and reward |
| Guardian defeated | +3 | same |
| Modules (Bank, Vault, Salvage, Magnet) | see §2.4 | more options |
| Leech at the Core | −3 | new pressure on hoarding |

### 6.2 The shop
- **Run pool:** at run start the game draws **16 modules** from the unlocked ones (6 Attack, 5 Boost, 5 Support; the Core's starting module always included), shown on the Run setup sheet and in pause. Only these appear in offers. Merges stay frequent; every run has its own "menu". The Daily/Weekly fix the pool for everyone.
- **4 offers** (5 with Scout); **owned modules weigh ×1.5** in the draw (merge help, deterministic).
- **Rarity by act:** act I C 70 · U 30 · act II C 50 · U 35 · R 13 · L 2 · act III C 40 · U 35 · R 20 · L 5 (%). L never offered if one is on the ring.
- **Cost by rarity:** C 3 · U 4–5 · R 6–7 · L 9.
- **Refresh** 1 Coin, +1 per further refresh in the same shop (same as today).
- **Lock** (new, free): tap the lock on a card to keep it into the next shop; locked cards are not refreshed. A replay command `Lock(offerIndex)`.
- **Sell** half of what was invested (Vault: everything). Undo as today.

### 6.3 Boons (pick 1 of 3 after each Guardian) — *superseded by Charms, `12` §3*
Run-wide rule changes, drawn from the Shop RNG stream. **Petal +1** is always one of the three while the ring has fewer than 8 petals (replaces the fixed extra-slot offer of `05` §5).
| Boon | Effect |
|---|---|
| Petal +1 | insert an empty petal where you choose (today's `BuySlot`, now free) |
| Fresh eyes | the first Refresh of every shop is free |
| Deep pockets | interest cap +3 |
| Big bang | Pulse damage ×2 |
| Wide view | +1 offer in every shop |
| Kinship | duplicates cost 1 less |
| Whetstone | Attack modules +2 flat damage |
| Long sight | all ranges +0.5 |
| Windfall | +10 Coins now |
| Second wind | full repair and +25 max Health |
| Lucky draw | rare and legendary chances ×1.5 |
| Quick hands | all cooldowns −10% |
| Double Pulse | the Pulse fires twice, the second at 50% |
Boons are part of the replay (`PickBoon(index)`), so Daily and ghosts stay fair.

### 6.4 Coin flow check (estimate, Standard Core, 18 waves)
Base 72 · interest ~45 · Guardians 9 · Flawless ~8 · elites ~10 · start 6 → **~150** (was ~135). Spending grows with more rarities and Lock. Target margin stays 10–50 Coins (`08` §1.2); the bot decides whether base income stays at 4.

### 6.5 Meta (outside the run)
- **Seeds** (Blueprints): 1 per wave, 3 per Guardian, Grade bonus — unchanged. With 22 new modules the Garden (Archive) grows from ~26 to **33 plantable modules + 3 Cores**: at the `08` §1.4 linear costs that is ~1,150 Seeds ≈ **75 runs**, still inside the 60–80 target. The new modules are spread across the Garden so the first 10 runs unlock one per 1–2 runs.
- Starting unlocked set (run 1): Emitter, Scatter, Needle, Amplifier, Lens, Overclock, Salvage, Bulwark, Bank, Frost + Standard Core — 10 modules, enough for a 16-pool from run 3 (until then the pool is everything unlocked).

## 8. Order of work (proposal)
1. Approve names (§1) and the systems (§3, §5, §6) — they change the game more than any single module.
2. Simulation: gates + bursts + beats, run pool + Lock + Boons, Flawless/elite rewards → bot probe (balance `0.4.0`).
3. Add modules and enemies in batches of 6, each with a behaviour test and a bot pass.
4. Blender: one parametric script per family (Attack, Boost, Support, Enemy) producing every shape of §2 and §4; renders feed the cards and the Codex.
